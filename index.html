Manchester uniteds history

Manchester United's history began in 1878 as Newton Heath LYR, a railway workers' team, nearly went bankrupt, and was saved by new owners who renamed them Manchester United in 1902, leading to their first league title in 1908 and move to Old Trafford in 1910. The club became famous under Sir Matt Busby, who rebuilt the team after the 1958 Munich Air Disaster to win the European Cup in 1968, and later achieved unparalleled success under Sir Alex Ferguson, winning numerous titles, including the historic 1999 Treble (League, FA Cup, Champions League).
